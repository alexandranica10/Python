Description
The Apprentice of Py-Kepp is a text-based interactive fiction game built in Python. You play as a junior wizard apprentice trying to survive a typical day of magical training. Throughout your day, you must manage your core stats—Energy, Health, and Gold Coins—by making strategic choices.
From negotiating with your enchanted toad alarm clock to stealing breakfast and navigating a scorching magical garden to harvest Glowing Mushrooms, every decision impacts your final stats. 

Features
Resource Management: Carefully balance your Energy, Health, and Gold Coins to survive the day.
Time Mechanics: Snoozing your magical alarm grants you extra energy but costs precious time. Oversleep, and you will face the wrath of the enchanted toad.
Risk vs. Reward: Choose how to spend your gold on breakfast, or take a 50/50 risk to steal an apple and save your money.
Exploration Loops: Navigate the enchanted garden in a continuous search loop until you successfully gather all 5 Glowing Mushrooms.

How to Play
Run the Python script in your terminal or command prompt.
Read the story prompts and check your current stats.
When prompted, type the number corresponding to your choice (e.g., 1, 2, or 3) and press Enter.
Keep an eye on your energy—every wrong turn in the enchanted garden drains it!

Requirements
Python 3.x

To run the game from your terminal:
python3 your_file_name.py
