# The Py Wizard Game 🧙‍♂️✨

A fun, interactive text-based adventure game written in Python. Step into the shoes of a magic student trying to survive a grueling day at **The Py Academy**. Manage your stats, make tough choices, and see if you have what it takes to finish the day as a Master Wizard!

## 📜 Storyline

You wake up to the sound of an enchanted toad croaking at 7:00 AM. From there, your choices dictate your fate. Will you snooze and risk being late? Will you steal an apple from the tavern or pay for a hearty breakfast? Can you survive the Haunted Bush in Herbology class, or successfully feed "Bitey" the Griffin? 

Every decision impacts your **Energy**, **Health**, and **Gold Coins**. Run out of energy or health, and you'll suffer Magical Exhaustion. Keep your stats high to win!

## 🎮 Features

* **Resource Management:** Balance your Energy, Health, and Gold Coins throughout the day.
* **Interactive Choices:** Every scene offers multiple paths with different risks and rewards.
* **Dynamic Loops:** Includes a foraging mini-game in Herbology class where you must explore different paths until you find enough Glowing Mushrooms.
* **Multiple Endings:** Three distinct endings based on your final stats:
  * 🏆 **Master Wizard** (High Health & Energy)
  * 🩹 **Barely Survived** (Low Health & Energy)
  * 💀 **Magical Exhaustion** (Zero Health or Energy)

## 🛠️ Prerequisites

* **Python 3.x** installed on your machine. No external libraries are required (uses the built-in `random` module).
